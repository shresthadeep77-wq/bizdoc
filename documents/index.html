Proforma invoices, purchase orders and quotations

One product list, five document types, and a paginated A4 layout that looks the same on screen, in the PDF and on paper.

Open the documents tab See the product catalogue

The five document types

Every type is built from the same product list, the same customer record and the same business header, so switching between them costs nothing. Each keeps its own running number series and its own default validity period.

Document types, their numbering and their validity defaults
TypeDefault number formatDated fieldNotes
Proforma invoice{YYYY}{####}Expiration date, 5 daysThe quote a buyer pays against before goods ship.
Purchase orderPO-{YYYY}-{####}Delivery by, 30 daysAddressed to a supplier rather than a customer.
QuotationQT-{YYYY}-{####}Valid until, 15 daysConverts straight into a proforma or a tax invoice.

Delivery noteDN-{YYYY}-{####}—Prints quantities with the prices hidden.

Numbering that stays in order

Number formats are yours to set per business and per type. {YYYY} becomes the year and {####} becomes a zero-padded counter that advances only when a document is actually saved — so an abandoned draft never burns a number and leaves a gap in your books.

One document becomes the next

A quotation that gets accepted does not need retyping. Convert it and the line items, discounts, customer and terms carry across into a proforma invoice or a tax invoice; a tax invoice converts on to a delivery note the same way. The new document takes the next number in its own series and keeps a reference back to where it came from.

Discounts, tax and totals

  • A percentage or fixed discount per line, per document, or inherited from the customer's saved rules.
  • Per-product taxable flags, so zero-rated items sit alongside taxable ones in the same document and the tax base still comes out right.
  • Freight, packing and other charges added as their own lines.
  • Totals in words, written out automatically for the amount payable.
  • A bank payment QR code generated from your account details, drawn into the PDF so it can be scanned straight off a printed page.

What comes out

  • PDF — a real paginated A4 document, with the header repeated and the totals block kept whole rather than split across a page break.
  • PNG — one image per page, for pasting into a chat.
  • Print — the browser's own dialogue, using the same layout.
  • WhatsApp — a share link with the document summary prefilled.

The preview is the document. What you see in the on-screen A4 preview is the same layout that is captured for the PDF and the PNG and sent to the printer, at the same page size. There is no second template that drifts out of step.
