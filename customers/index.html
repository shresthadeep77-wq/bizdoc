A customer list that fills in the paperwork

Enter a company once. Every document you raise afterwards picks up its address, tax number and agreed discounts on its own.

Open the customers tab See what gets raised against them

What a customer record holds

  • Client number, company name and contact name.
  • Phone, email and billing address.
  • VAT or PAN number, printed on every tax document raised for that account.
  • Any number of discount rules, applied automatically to matching line items.

Pick the company once when you start a document and all of it drops into the header — no copying an address out of an old file and no stale tax number going out on a printed invoice.

Discount rules that follow the catalogue

A rule is a category path and a percentage. Point one at a whole branch — say Fittings — and every product under it gets that discount for this customer. Point another at Fittings › Brass and the deeper, more specific rule wins for brass fittings while the broader one still covers the rest.

That means a negotiated price list is entered once, as a handful of rules, instead of being remembered line by line on every document. You can still override the discount on an individual line when a one-off deal calls for it.

The customer report

Every account prints a dated statement covering the documents raised against it:

  • Identity block with client number, tax number and full contact details.
  • Total billed and total discount given, with the average document value.
  • A count of documents by status, so drafts and cancellations are visible instead of quietly inflating the totals.
  • The product mix by value, ranked, showing what this customer actually buys.
  • Every document listed with its number, date, type and amount.

Cancelled documents are excluded from the money figures but still counted in the status breakdown. The report exports as a PDF from the same preview you see.

Getting a customer list in and out

Customers import and export as CSV, with a preview step before anything is written so a bad column mapping is caught rather than merged in. A blank template with the exact expected columns is downloadable from the same menu, which is usually the fastest way to move a list across from a spreadsheet.

All of this is local. Customer names, addresses and tax numbers live in your browser on this device and are not sent anywhere. See how the data is stored.
