This page describes exactly what the app does with what you type. It is short because the app does very little.

There is no server

PI & PO Maker is a static page. Once it has loaded, there is no backend it talks to, no API it posts to and nowhere for your documents to be sent. Businesses, customers, products and documents are written to localStorage in your own browser, under a single key, on the device you are using.

No account, no analytics, no cookies

  • There is no sign-up and no login.
  • No analytics, telemetry, session recording or error reporting is loaded.
  • The app sets no cookies and no tracking identifiers.
  • There are no third-party advertising or social scripts on the page.

The two things that do leave your device

Both are off until you start them, and neither runs in the background.

Google Drive backup

If — and only if — you press Back up to Google Drive, the app loads Google's sign-in script, asks for your permission, and writes one JSON file into your own Drive. It requests the drive.file scope, which is the narrowest one available: it can see and change only the file it created itself, and has no access to anything else in your Drive. Restoring reads that same file back. Google's own handling of it is covered by their privacy policy, not this one.

WhatsApp sharing

The WhatsApp share button opens WhatsApp with a message prefilled. Nothing is sent until you choose a recipient and press send yourself.

The other side of that bargain

Because nothing is on a server, nobody can recover your data for you. If you clear your browsing data, uninstall the browser, or lose the device, the records go with it. Storage is also per browser and per device — Chrome on your laptop and Safari on your phone are two separate sets of data.

So take backups. Export a backup file somewhere safe on a schedule that matches how much work you would hate to retype.

Deleting everything

Clearing site data for this page in your browser's settings removes every trace of it. There is no copy anywhere else to ask us to delete, and no account to close.
